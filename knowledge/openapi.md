Ilm Ke Dunya OpenAPI gives developers access to structured Islamic knowledge
through a public, machine-readable interface. It is designed for web apps, AI
systems, research tools, and learning experiences that need dependable content.

## Included domains

The API covers key areas such as:

- Quran and surah information
- Hadith collections and references
- Duas and adhkar
- Seerah and contextual guidance
- Related Islamic reference material

## Why it exists

Rather than leaving knowledge trapped in individual interfaces, the OpenAPI
layer exposes a standard pathway for integration. This allows teams to build
products, tools, and experiences on top of the same trusted source.

## Current status

The OpenAPI surface is live and ready for developer access. It provides a
practical path for software integration while the wider platform continues to
grow.
