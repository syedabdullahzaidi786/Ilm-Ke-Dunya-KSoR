Ilm Ke Dunya covers a focused set of Islamic knowledge domains that are most
valuable for learning, reference, and digital access.

## Core coverage areas

### Quran
Quran content includes surah information, verses, translations, and structured
reference material.

### Hadith
Hadith collections support study, reference, and reliable access to narration
metadata and related guidance.

### Duas and adhkar
The platform includes supplications and daily remembrance content for practical,
faith-based use.

### Seerah and guidance
Contextual knowledge and guidance resources help users understand broader Islamic
principles in a structured and readable way.

## Overall goal

The purpose is to provide a dependable, coherent body of Islamic knowledge that
works across reading experiences, API access, and AI-assisted workflows.
